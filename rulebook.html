The room creator is the host. The host can run round controls, but never decides a player’s private strategy.

01 — SETUP

Claim a country and build a fortune

Scoring convention: After the third calculation, the game ends and the table displays ranked final balances. The app does not declare a winner automatically—your table chooses the winner, then the host can restart the room for a new game.
  1. Enter a unique commander name. The game assigns you one available country.
  2. Each country has Agriculture, Oil, and Mines multipliers. Lean into your strongest resources.
  3. Players begin with 0 coins. The purchase control requests 100 coins at a time. Approved coins plus pending requests may never exceed 500 coins.
  4. The first authenticated player to join the room is its host. Only that player can access host controls.

Random round multipliers

Fresh economic conditions every round: At the start of each round, every country receives a new random allocation of ×1, ×2, and ×3 across Agriculture, Oil, and Mines. Check the live multiplier panel before investing; the server uses those same values for payouts and skirmishes.

02 — ROUND FLOW

Every calculation follows the same six steps

1

Deal proficiency cards

The host deals exactly two cards to every player. Cards can create an economic advantage, an alliance, or a conflict.

2

Draw a Global Condition

After cards are dealt, the host draws one condition. Its effect lasts until this round is calculated.

3

Fund and invest

Request coins, allocate them among your three fields, and leave any amount you may need for trades.

4

Negotiate or act

Make trades, play cards, form alliances, or launch skirmishes when their requirements are met.

5

Lock and mark ready

Lock field investments, then mark yourself ready. Locked investments cannot be edited for that round.

6

Calculate

Once every seated player has locked investments and marked ready, the host closes the round and payouts are calculated. After Round 3, the game ends and ranked placements appear for the table to choose a winner.

03 — ECONOMY & INVESTMENTS

Turn investments into round income

Field payout ⌊ investment × country multiplier ⌋

Calculate Agriculture, Oil, and Mines separately, then add them together. Unallocated coins stay in your wallet, but do not generate field income. After the round payout, field allocations reset and you allocate again next round.

🏦 Coin purchases

Use Buy 100 Coins to submit a request. The host approves or rejects each one separately. Pending requests count toward the 500-coin purchase cap, so you can have at most five 100-coin requests approved or awaiting a decision at once. A rejected request frees 100 coins of that allowance. Payouts, loans, and transfers can still take a balance higher.

🏦 Banker loans

The Banker card lets you borrow up to 250 coins and is consumed when used. At round end, the full repayment is collected from your available balance and current-round income: original principal plus 20% interest. A 100-coin loan therefore requires 120 coins. If you cannot cover the full amount, all available coins are collected and the unpaid balance remains as an active loan for the next round.

Trades

A bilateral trade exchanges coins from unallocated cash or a selected field for coins in the other player’s selected field or wallet. Sending an offer reserves your adjusted offer in escrow immediately. If it is rejected, cannot settle, or remains unresolved at round calculation, escrow is returned.

  • Pandemic: all Agriculture, Oil, and Mines multipliers are overridden to ×1 for the round.
  • Economic Recession: received trade income is rounded down after a 20% reduction.
  • Merchant: adds 10% to trade income before any Recession reduction.

04 — PROFICIENCY CARDS

Use each card at the right moment

🏦

Banker

Borrow up to 250 coins. The card is consumed.

🏛️

President

After locking investments, invite one or two powers into a Mega-Merger. Every invited power must approve.

🎖️

General

Gain two skirmish attacks this round instead of the normal one.

🕵️

Spy

Publish a visible Spy operation against an agreement selected in the card dialog. In the current build, it does not reverse a live trade escrow. Cold War prevents this card from being used and does not consume it.

💰

Merchant

Gain +10% trade income for the current round.

☢️

Atomic Bomb

After locking investments, destroy half of one selected field’s current investment for another power.

Alliances

A President Mega-Merger joins two or three consenting powers for the current round. For each resource, the shared pool is calculated from the initiator’s locked investment: max(150, initiator investment × member count + 150). Each member then applies their own country multiplier to that shared pool and receives an equal rounded-down share. This is not a sum of every member’s investments. When a Mega-Merger forms, unaligned powers can invite one or two partners into a mutually approved Counter-Union using the same formula. Alliances clear after the round calculation.

05 — CONFLICT

Locked investments can become battle power

You must lock your investments before a skirmish or an Atomic Bomb can be used. Each player may launch one skirmish per round; a General increases that limit to two.

⚔️ Skirmish

Choose a rival and field. Your attack power is your investment in that field multiplied by your country’s multiplier. A higher attack wins seized coins; a tie changes nothing. If you lose, your investment in the attacked field is wiped out.

🏛️ Alliance skirmish

Only the country that initiated a confirmed Mega-Merger or Counter-Union may launch one coalition skirmish each round. It may target a locked, unaligned country or the opposing confirmed alliance. Both sides use their live contested resource, country multiplier, and any active Global Condition. The higher power takes the other side’s entire current resource into its own pool or field; a tie transfers nothing.

☢️ Atomic strike

Choose a rival and one field. The Atomic Bomb immediately destroys half of that target field’s current investment, rounding up the amount destroyed. It is a precision disruption tool, not a coin transfer.

06 — GLOBAL CONDITIONS

Adapt to the world state

📉

Economic Recession

Trade income is reduced by 20% this round.

🌡️

Global Warming

Agriculture and Oil payouts are each reduced by 10% this round.

🦠

Pandemic

All Agriculture, Oil, and Mines multipliers become ×1 this round.

🕊️

Cold War

Spy cards cannot be played this round.

Final reminder: Watch the round announcements and result popups. They confirm trade settlements, battle results, alliances, global conditions, and the final payout details.
