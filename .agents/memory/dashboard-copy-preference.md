---
name: Dashboard table layout
description: The user's preference for concise game-card and round-control panels, latest announcements at table center, and square badges without “YOU.”
---

In the player dashboard, do not repeat a tab name as a visible heading or add static helper instructions where the tab or interactive elements already provide the context. Remove the “NEXT MOVE” status item and its dynamic next-action text, but keep the actual investment-lock button. The Game Cards panel should keep its accessible “Game Cards” label and live content, but omit the visible “ROUND DECK” kicker and the tagline about live conditions and available proficiency cards. The player table should not show a generic ticker, condition tag, round counter, or branded “WORLD WAR TABLE” center box. Show the latest Round Announcement in the table center while keeping the full announcement history in Review. Keep player badges square and preserve their information and accessible names, but do not add a “YOU” label to the player's own badge. Place the Round Closure Consensus card directly below the live table in the Act tab and keep only the Ready for Next Round button inside it; host-only round controls belong outside that card. The separate TV feed can retain its brand and live status display.

**Why:** the user requested a less cluttered player dashboard, removal of the next-action status item, concise Game Cards copy, latest announcements at table center without the brand box, square player badges without “YOU,” and a Ready-only consensus card.

**How to apply:** Keep only the latest announcement in the player-table center and the full feed in Review. Preserve per-player details, keep the Ready button below the live table with host controls outside its card, and leave the separate TV broadcast display intact.
