---
name: Act action independence
description: Field Trade and Field Battle availability once the table has completed Prepare.
---

After every seated commander completes Prepare, each commander may choose a Field Trade or a Field Battle in either order. Completing, proposing, accepting, rejecting, or skipping a trade must never unlock or block a Field Battle.

**Why:** The game’s Act phase is a choice between independent tactical actions, not a required action sequence.

**How to apply:** Gate both actions on the completed table-wide Prepare state and the acting commander not being marked ready. Retain their own limits and legitimate requirements, such as a battle allowance, valid locked targets, and Banker repayment, but never use trade state as a battle prerequisite.
