---
name: Act action independence
description: Act action availability after an individual commander completes Prepare.
---

After all seated commanders have locked investments and the global event is drawn, each commander may choose a Field Trade or Field Battle. Completing, proposing, accepting, rejecting, or skipping a trade must never unlock or block a Field Battle. Marking ready still closes that commander's Act actions.

**Why:** The user requires a table-wide Prepare gate for Act actions, while keeping Field Trade and Field Battle independent of each other.

**How to apply:** Gate both actions on every seated commander's locked investments, the global event being drawn, and the acting commander not being marked ready. Retain per-player limits and legitimate requirements, such as a battle allowance, valid locked targets, and Banker repayment. Never use trade state as a Field Battle prerequisite.
