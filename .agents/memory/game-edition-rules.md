---
name: Game edition rules
description: Product definition for the planned Simple and Advanced game editions.
---

The Simple Edition uses the same rules and cards as Advanced except that it does not deal the Banker or President cards. Without the President card, no Mega-Merger can be formed; because Counter-Union only responds to a Mega-Merger, Counter-Union is also unavailable in Simple. Banker loan controls are hidden in Simple rather than displayed as disabled.

**Why:** The user defined Simple as a reduced-card version rather than a separate ruleset, while keeping Global Conditions and all other gameplay unchanged.

**How to apply:** Keep the two editions on the same core game flow. Scope the card deck, Banker UI/actions, and alliance-related UI/server actions by edition; do not remove unrelated cards or Global Conditions from Simple.
