---
name: Game edition rules
description: Product definition for the planned Simple and Advanced game editions.
---

The Simple Edition deals one proficiency card per player per round; Advanced deals two. Simple excludes Banker and President cards, but keeps General, Spy, Merchant, and Atomic Bomb. Atomic Bomb remains playable during Pandemic in Simple, while Pandemic still deactivates it in Advanced. Without the President card, no Mega-Merger can be formed; because Counter-Union only responds to a Mega-Merger, Counter-Union is also unavailable in Simple. Banker loan controls are hidden in Simple rather than displayed as disabled.

**Why:** The user defined Simple as a lighter, one-card variant with a targeted Atomic Bomb exception, while retaining the remaining Global Conditions and core gameplay.

**How to apply:** Keep the two editions on the same core game flow. Scope card count, card deck, Atomic Bomb Pandemic behavior, Banker UI/actions, and alliance-related UI/server actions by edition; do not remove unrelated cards or Global Conditions from Simple.
